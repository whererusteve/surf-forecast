    How this works & surf respectfully

    Every dot is a stretch of coast found in NOAA seafloor depth data (ETOPO 2022). For each one we worked out which swell directions can physically reach it past islands and headlands, which way it faces, and how the seafloor shoals. Live swell and wind (Open-Meteo) are scored against that geography, hour by hour.

    Scores are a model, not a surf report: the depth grid is ~450 m, so it can’t see individual reefs, rocks or sandbars, and in parts of BC a dot can sit a few hundred metres off the real shoreline. Use the satellite view to scout, and expect plenty of misses.

    Much of this coast is the territory of First Nations, including reserve land and sites that need permission to visit. Find out whose land you're on before you go. Many spots are hours from help, in cold water with no cell service, so go with a partner and tell someone your plan.

    Data: Open-Meteo, NOAA ETOPO 2022, Fisheries and Oceans Canada, NOAA CO-OPS. Imagery: Esri.
